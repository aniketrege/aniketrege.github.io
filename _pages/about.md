Hello There! I'm a Ph.D student in Computer Sciences at UW-Madison, fortunate to be jointly advised by [Ramya Vinayak](https://ramyakv.github.io/) and [Yong Jae Lee](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~yongjaelee/).

🚨 *I'm on the market for **research internships** in Spring/Summer 2027, focused on agents, RL post-training, and multimodal reasoning!*

These days I spend most of my time thinking about: 
* **Long-horizon multimodal reasoning agents** - how do we train agents to plan, search, and reflect on their decisions consistently over long reasoning chains? What kind of datasets and evals are needed?
* **Heterogeneous and diverse preference learning** - humans are extremely diverse, and generative models are often [trained not to be](https://www.tuananhle.co.uk/notes/reverse-forward-kl.html). How can we do better at catering to such pluralistic preferences?
* **Broad Interests**: elastic embedding models, multi-agent systems, information retrieval, efficient+deployable ML.

Previously, I was a masters student at the University of Washington, Seattle advised by [Prof. Ali Farhadi](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~ali/) and mentored by [Aditya Kusupati](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~kusupati/). My MS research focused on large-scale elastic and adaptive representation learning (Matryoshka 🪆) and web-scale search. 

I was also fortunate to spend time in the industry as a research intern at Google DeepMind (with [Gagan Madan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gagan-madan/)), Meta (with [Hyo Jin Kim](https://www.cs.unc.edu/~hyojin/) and [Yuning Chai](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chaiyuning/)), and NVIDIA (with [Nikolai Smolyanskiy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KxfefwgAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Stan Birchfield](https://research.nvidia.com/person/stan-birchfield)), and three years as a research engineer at Samsung Research.
