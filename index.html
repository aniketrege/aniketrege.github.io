Hello There! I’m a Ph.D student in Computer Sciences at UW-Madison, fortunate to be jointly advised by Ramya Vinayak and Yong Jae Lee.

🚨 I’m on the market for research internships in Spring/Summer 2027, focused on agents, RL post-training, and multimodal reasoning!

These days I spend most of my time thinking about:

  • Long-horizon multimodal reasoning agents - how do we train agents to plan, search, and reflect on their decisions consistently over long reasoning chains? What kind of datasets and evals are needed?
  • Heterogeneous and diverse preference learning - humans are extremely diverse, and generative models are often trained not to be. How can we do better at catering to such pluralistic preferences?
  • Broad Interests: elastic embedding models, multi-agent systems, information retrieval, efficient+deployable ML.

Previously, I was a masters student at the University of Washington, Seattle advised by Prof. Ali Farhadi and mentored by Aditya Kusupati. My MS research focused on large-scale elastic and adaptive representation learning (Matryoshka 🪆) and web-scale search.

I was also fortunate to spend time in the industry as a research intern at Google DeepMind (with Gagan Madan), Meta (with Hyo Jin Kim and Yuning Chai), and NVIDIA (with Nikolai Smolyanskiy and Stan Birchfield), and three years as a research engineer at Samsung Research.

If you are an undergrad/masters student interested in working with me, send me an email (I’ll do my best to reply)!
